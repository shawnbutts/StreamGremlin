Asking StreamGremlin…

Key

The key shows the button’s colour and icon. A dim key’s button starts nothing that’s on.

Counters are the variables you choose in StreamGremlin, on Connections, “What your Stream Deck can use”.

It only shows: a press does nothing. “?” means StreamGremlin doesn’t know.

Switches are the workflows, timers and chat lock you choose in StreamGremlin, on Connections, “What your Stream Deck can use”. Lit is on.

Runs it now, whatever starts it otherwise, if it’s on (a dim key’s workflow is off). A workflow that starts from a viewer’s message gets no viewer or message from here; a button on Live is usually better. Choose workflows in StreamGremlin, on Connections, “What your Stream Deck can use”.

Lit while it’s on air; a press switches OBS to it. Allow scene keys in StreamGremlin, on Connections, “What your Stream Deck can use”.

Countdowns are Clock and timers overlays you choose in StreamGremlin, on Connections, “What your Stream Deck can use”.

Shows when Twitch’s next ad is due and how many snoozes are left; a press snoozes it. Allow it in StreamGremlin, on Connections, “What your Stream Deck can use”.
