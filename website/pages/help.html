Your data

Everything StreamGremlin keeps (workflows, runs, events, variables and settings) is in one folder on your computer. Help in the app shows exactly where. Sign-ins and passwords are in your system’s keyring, not in that folder. Nothing is sent anywhere but the services you connect.

  • Windows: %LocalAppData%\StreamGremlin
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/StreamGremlin

The streamgremlin command does what the app does, from a terminal: try streamgremlin status, streamgremlin workflows or streamgremlin help.

Something went wrong?

Tell whoever invited you to the beta. The most useful report has:

  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead (a screenshot helps);
  • the version (Help in the app shows it), and Windows or macOS;
  • the log files from the logs folder in your data folder. Logs never contain your passwords or sign-ins, but they do have chat names and messages from your stream.

Found a security problem? Please see Security and report it privately.
