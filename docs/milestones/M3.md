### Live check (user, macOS and Windows; real Twitch)

1. Build: `make verify`.
2. **Giveaways** (in the sidebar): open a practice giveaway. On **Events**, send test chat commands `!join` as two or three viewers. They appear as entries, and "In chat" shows what it would say. Draw, and **Check the draws**: ✓. Cancel.
3. Open a real giveaway with **Winners must claim** (60 seconds). In Twitch chat it says how to enter. From a second Twitch account (a friend, or another account in a private browser window; not your bot, whose chat StreamGremlin ignores), type `!join`: chat says they're in within a few seconds. Your own `!join` doesn't count.
4. Draw: chat names the winner and says to type `!claim`. From that account, type `!claim`: chat says so. **End the giveaway**: chat congratulates them.
5. **Workflows → Commands:** while a giveaway runs, `!join` is listed as the giveaway's.
6. Terminal: `./bin/streamgremlin giveaways`, then `./bin/streamgremlin giveaways verify ID` with the id it lists: ✓.
