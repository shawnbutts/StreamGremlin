# M8: Protection

Protection against hate raids, spam bots and follow bots, built from Twitch's documented API and the streamer's own lists, without a bot list of StreamGremlin's (D-104). It comes before the release work.

**Status:** decided 2026-11-14 (D-104). Slices 1 and 2 done; slice 3 built.

M8 is done when the user has used it on a real stream on macOS and Windows, and the result is written below.

## What acts on its own

- **The chat filters,** as now, with the new rules (slice 4).
- **The raid guard** alerts by default; it locks chat only if the streamer chooses that, and never unlocks on its own.
- **Bulk bans and follow-bot blocks** always show what they'll do and ask first.

## Slices

| # | Slice | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shield Mode and a panic button: Shield Mode's state on Live with a switch; "Shield Mode turns on / off" triggers and a step; **Lock chat** on Live (Shield Mode, followers-only and emote-only at once, and one button to undo it as it was); a "Lock chat when I go offline" template (D-105) | Done 2026-11-14 |
| 2 | Bulk bans from a file: import a list (one name or user ID a line; CSV and other tools' exports), see what it will do first (found, already banned, not found, you or your mods left alone), then ban in the background at Twitch's pace, resumable across restarts, with a reason; progress and a report on a new **Ban lists** page; undo an import; export Twitch's ban list to a file ([ADR-0049](../adr/0049-ban-lists.md), D-106) | Done 2026-11-14 |
| 3 | The raid guard: a burst of new chatters (first seen by StreamGremlin, or accounts younger than N days) within a short time, or right after a raid, starts "A raid looks suspicious"; it alerts on Live by default, or locks chat (slice 1) if chosen (D-107) | Built: live check next |
| 4 | New-account and ASCII-art rules in the chat filters: a first-time chatter whose account is younger than N days is deleted, timed out or warned; ASCII art (block and Braille characters) caught like symbols | Proposed |
| 5 | Follow-bot review: after a stream, follows that came in bursts from brand-new accounts that never chatted, listed for review; **Block…** asks first. Whether blocking removes a follow isn't documented, so the screen says only what Twitch does document, until a live check shows more | Proposed |

Each slice checks Twitch's current docs first, records scopes, limits and anything uncertain in Twitch's capability manifest, and asks the streamer to sign in again when it needs a new permission. Each has its screen, tests (with mocktwitch), an acceptance test through the real binaries, and a live check on macOS and Windows.

## Left out

- **A shared bot list kept by StreamGremlin:** it needs a server of ours, and guesses. The streamer's own lists, from files, instead (slice 2).
- **View bots:** Twitch gives no way to see who is watching.
- **YouTube:** no Shield Mode, members-only or follower switch in its API (D-100).

## Slice 1: Shield Mode and Lock chat

**Built** (D-105):
- **Live → Chat modes:** **Lock chat** turns on Shield Mode, emote-only and followers-only (followed at least 10 minutes) at once; while locked it says so, with **Unlock chat**, which puts back only what the lock changed. **Shield Mode** turns it on and off by itself, and says unknown when Twitch can't say.
- **Workflows:** the trigger **Shield Mode turns on or off** (from anywhere, with who did it); the steps **Turn Shield Mode on or off** and **Lock or unlock chat**; templates **Shield Mode on when I go offline**, **Shield Mode off when I go live** and **!lock and !unlock chat** (mods and you).
- **CLI:** `mod shield on|off`, `mod lock`, `mod unlock`; `mod modes` shows Shield Mode and the lock.
- Every one is in the moderation record, sent once; Twitch sign-in asks for one more permission (Shield Mode).

**Checked:**
- Twitch: Shield Mode read, turned on (with when), turned off from Twitch's site, each an event naming the moderator; the request's channel id in the query.
- The record: shield_mode sent once and refused without on; a lock turning everything on and recording what was there; a second lock refused; an unlock putting back only what changed (slow mode kept, a longer follow time kept, Shield Mode left on if it was); unlock with nothing locked refused; a part refused said beside what worked; Twitch not answering is unknown and still unlockable, turning it all off.
- The steps and templates in the editor and on test events; Live's buttons, words and unknowns, with accessibility checks.
- Acceptance through the real binary and mocktwitch: Shield Mode on and off, lock and unlock with slow mode kept, twice refused, the history, Shield Mode from Twitch's site as an event, and the offline template turning Shield Mode on.
- `make verify` and `make windows`.

### Live check for slice 1 (user, macOS and Windows)

1. On **Connections**, sign in to Twitch again: it asks for one more permission (Shield Mode). **Home** says so until you do.
2. In Twitch's Mod View, look at Shield Mode's settings, so you know what it does on your channel.
3. On **Live**, turn on **Slow** (say 10 seconds), then press **Lock chat**. On Twitch: Shield Mode is on, chat is emote-only and followers-only (10 minutes). Live says chat is locked.
4. Press **Unlock chat**. Shield Mode, emote-only and followers-only go off; slow mode is still on.
5. Press **Shield Mode** on Live, then turn it off again in Twitch's Mod View. Within a few seconds, Live shows it off, and **Events** shows both changes.
6. Add **!lock and !unlock chat** from **Templates → Moderation**. From a mod account, type `!lock`, then `!unlock`.
7. Optional: add **Shield Mode on when I go offline**, end a stream, and see Shield Mode on.
8. On Windows too: steps 3 to 5.

**Result:** passed on macOS and Windows, 2026-11-14. The user: "live checks look good."

## Slice 2: ban lists from files

**Built** (D-106, [ADR-0049](../adr/0049-ban-lists.md)):
- **Ban lists** screen (after Chat filters): import a list from a file (**Choose file…**), or **Copy Twitch's ban list**. A checked list shows how many it will ban, how many are already banned, how many weren't found, who's left alone, and a sample. **Start banning** confirms, optionally unbanning names removed from a newer file or banning again names unbanned by hand. While running: progress, the pace, the estimated finish, **Pause** and **Resume**, and when it waits while you're live. Done: **Check against Twitch**, **Save to a file…**, **Undo…** (asks first), **Forget…**. Settings per list: wait while live, leave viewers with points alone.
- **CLI:** `bans`, `bans import NAME FILE [-reason]`, `bans copy NAME`, `bans show NAME [STATE]`, `bans confirm NAME [-unban-removed] [-reban-by-hand]`, `bans pause|resume|compare NAME`, `bans undo NAME -yes`, `bans save NAME FILE [STATE]`, `bans forget NAME -yes`.
- **Engine:** one runner, one request at a time; Twitch's rate-limit headers read, 200 points kept spare; each name's state saved before and after its request.

**Checked:**
- Reading: lines, CSV headers and columns, ids, `@`, quotes, comments, a BOM, names that aren't names, a line too long.
- Checking: found, not found, by login and by id, already banned, you, mods, VIPs, viewers with points; nothing banned before confirming.
- Banning: the reason; the spare kept and the reset waited for; "too many" waited out and sent again; an unanswered ban settled from Twitch's list and never sent twice; a sign-in problem pausing the list in words with nothing marked refused, then resumed.
- Syncing: a newer file asking only about new names, removed names offered and unbanned only when asked, a hand unban found and left alone unless asked, undo lifting only the list's own bans, forgetting unbanning nobody; copying Twitch's list, paged.
- Restarts: a crash part way carrying on, and waiting while live.
- The screen (counts, sample, confirm options, progress and finish, pause and resume, undo asking first, waiting while live) with accessibility checks; the API and its errors.
- Acceptance through the real binary and mocktwitch with Twitch's rate limit on: 300 generated names checked and banned, the engine killed part way and started again with nobody banned twice and no "too many", a hand unban found, a newer file banning only its two new names, saving, and undo leaving only the ban made elsewhere.
- `make verify` and `make windows`.

### Live check for slice 2 (user, macOS and Windows)

You'll need a few Twitch accounts you can ban (test or alt accounts: not real viewers).

1. Make a text file with those accounts' names, one a line, plus a made-up name and your own channel's name. On **Ban lists**, name it, **Choose file…**, **Import**.
2. It shows how many it will ban, that the made-up name wasn't found, and that you're left alone. Check on Twitch that nobody is banned yet.
3. **Start banning**. Watch them get banned on Twitch (Mod View → Banned users), with the reason "Ban list: (its name)".
4. With a longer list (or while it's running), quit StreamGremlin part way, start it again: it carries on, and nobody is banned twice.
5. Unban one of them on Twitch, then **Check against Twitch**: it shows one unbanned by hand.
6. Add one more name to the file, take one out, and import it again under the same name: it checks only the new one, and offers to unban the one taken out. **Start banning** without ticking: only the new name is banned.
7. **Save to a file…**, then **Undo…**: everyone the list banned is unbanned, and the one you unbanned by hand stays as it is.
8. **Copy Twitch's ban list** into a new list, and **Save to a file…**.
9. On Windows too: steps 1 to 3, and 7.

**Result:** passed on macOS and Windows, 2026-11-14, with the decluttered Chat modes panel (D-105). The user: "live checks look good."

## Slice 3: the raid guard

**Built** (D-107):
- **Chat filters → Raid guard:** warn me (the default), warn me and lock chat, or off; how many first-time chatters, or Twitch accounts younger than how many days, within how many seconds is a burst; how long to watch more closely after a raid; **Test the warning**.
- **Live:** a warning with the count, the accounts and the raid it followed, and **Lock chat**, **Ban these N accounts…** (asks first; a ban list that bans at once and can be undone) and **Dismiss**.
- **Workflows:** the trigger **A raid looks suspicious**, with the count, names, window, the raider and whether chat was locked.
- **CLI:** `raidguard`, `raidguard set mode=… first=… new=… days=… window=… after-raid=…`, `raidguard test`, `raidguard dismiss`.

**Checked:**
- Below the count nothing; at it one warning with the accounts; YouTube counting toward first-time chatters.
- You, mods, VIPs, subscribers, members and known viewers never counted, and an account never counted twice.
- The cooldown; brand-new Twitch accounts by their age, asked once each; half the count after a raid, naming the raider.
- Locking only in lock mode; a test warning locking nothing and stored as a test; off counting nothing; settings kept across a restart and refused when wrong.
- A ban list from names: checked, banned at once though live, the broadcaster left alone; without start it waits while live and then for a confirm.
- Live's warning (words, names, banning only Twitch accounts after asking, dismissing) and Chat filters' settings and test, with accessibility checks; the API.
- Acceptance through the real binary and mocktwitch: a regular and an old account not counted, a raid then three brand-new accounts warning and locking chat, the lock in the record, the raiders banned from names, no second warning within the cooldown.
- `make verify` and `make windows`.

### Live check for slice 3 (user, macOS and Windows)

You'll need two or three alt Twitch accounts that haven't chatted on your channel since you started using StreamGremlin. A raid from a friend's channel is optional.

1. On **Chat filters → Raid guard**, press **Test the warning**: Live shows a test warning (nothing locks).
2. Set **First-time chatters in a burst** to 2 and **Within** to 60 seconds, keep **Warn me on Live**, and **Save**.
3. Chat once from each of two alt accounts within a minute. Live warns "2 first-time chatters in 60 seconds", naming them. **Events** shows "Raid guard: …".
4. Press **Ban these 2 accounts…**, confirm: they're banned on Twitch, and **Ban lists** shows the list. **Undo…** there unbans them.
5. Choose **Warn me and lock chat**, wait 5 minutes (the cooldown), and chat from two more new accounts (or the same two after **Forget** on Viewers): chat locks. **Unlock chat** puts it back.
6. Put the settings back (8, 30 seconds) or turn it off.
7. On Windows too: steps 1 to 3.

**Result:** not yet.
