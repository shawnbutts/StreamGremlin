**Fix between slices 1 and 2 (D-023):** workflows never answer their own messages.
- **Unit test:** a workflow's message is skipped, while what you type in the app or in Twitch, and a viewer's message, are stored. The tracker matches by text before Twitch answers and by id after, and forgets failed and lost sends.
- **Acceptance test:** an "answer any chat" workflow sending as you answers a viewer once (55 times without the fix), and still answers you.

Counters aren't a slice: saved variables are counters (D-024). F2 begins with slice 3; after alert overlays come channel point rewards, then Hype Trains, polls, predictions, First Words and ads.
