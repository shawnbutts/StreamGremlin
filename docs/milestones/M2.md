**Fix between slices 1 and 2 (D-023):** workflows never answer their own messages.
- **Unit test:** a workflow's message is skipped, while what you type in the app or in Twitch, and a viewer's message, are stored. The tracker matches by text before Twitch answers and by id after, and forgets failed and lost sends.

8. Try **On a schedule** with an example (such as `@hourly`) and save: it shows the next time on the hour.
9. **Delete** Socials: the dialog names the workflow that's kept. Confirm.
