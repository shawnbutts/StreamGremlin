**Fix between slices 1 and 2 (D-023):** workflows never answer their own messages.
- **Unit test:** a workflow's message is skipped, while what you type in the app or in Twitch, and a viewer's message, are stored. The tracker matches by text before Twitch answers and by id after, and forgets failed and lost sends.
- **Acceptance test:** an "answer any chat" workflow sending as you answers a viewer once (55 times without the fix), and still answers you.

Counters aren't a slice: saved variables are counters (D-024). F2 begins with slice 3; next are sounds, alert overlays, channel point rewards, then Hype Trains, polls, predictions, First Words and ads.

### Live check to do (user, macOS and Windows)

1. Build: `make verify`. Open the app. There's a new **Timers** screen (Ctrl/⌘+5); Variables, Help and Settings moved down one.
2. **New timer:** name it "Socials", every 1 minute, chat lines 0, "Only while I'm live" off, On. Save. It says no workflow uses it yet, and the list says when it goes off next.
3. Press **Make a workflow**. The editor opens with "A timer goes off" and Socials chosen. Type a message such as `Follow me! (reminder {counter})`, save, and turn the workflow on (on the Workflows screen).
4. Press **Run now** on Socials: the message appears in your Twitch chat with reminder 1. Within a minute or so it posts again with 2, by itself.
5. **Edit** Socials: set chat lines to 2 and save. When its time comes, the list says "Waiting for chat: 0 of 2 lines". Your own messages don't count. Have a viewer (or a second account) send two messages: it posts.
6. Set "Only while I'm live" on. While OBS isn't streaming, it says turns pass unless you're live, and nothing is posted. Start streaming in OBS: it posts on its turns.
7. Make a second workflow: a `!nosocials` command for mods, with the step **Turn a timer on or off** (Socials, off). Type `!nosocials` in chat: Socials shows off on the Timers screen.
8. Try **On a schedule** with an example (such as `@hourly`) and save: it shows the next time on the hour.
9. **Delete** Socials: the dialog names the workflow that's kept. Confirm.
10. Terminal: `./bin/streamgremlin timers set socials -name Socials -every 2m`, then `./bin/streamgremlin timers` and `./bin/streamgremlin timers run socials`.
11. On Windows: steps 2 to 4 and 9.

**Results (2026-11-08, the user, macOS and Windows):** all steps passed ("live checks look good").

7. Go live: Events shows "Live on Twitch". A timer set to "only while I'm live" now goes off. Change your title or category on Twitch: Events shows it, and a quote added with `!quoteadd` takes the category.
8. Terminal: `./bin/streamgremlin twitch` lists the events and your channel.
