# ADR-0024: Ads

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **Based on:** StreamWisp ADR-0022 (the ad break trigger and the schedule) and ADR-0023 (requests and confirmed breaks kept apart), with D-036 and D-038 there. Changed: one service owns both; there's no Ad Break Director, ad policies or intermissions (Later in FEATURES); the Home card is the only screen.

## Context

FEATURES lists, for F2: the ad break begins trigger; the ad schedule, snooze and start commercial steps; a "next ad / snooze" card on Home; and ad requests and confirmed breaks recorded separately. Ads need an Affiliate or Partner channel, so by D-039 they're built and checked against mocktwitch.

**Twitch's docs** (the API reference, read 2026-11-09):
- **Start Commercial** (`channel:edit:commercial`):
  - the broadcaster only (not editors or moderators), Partner or Affiliate, live;
  - up to 180 seconds asked; Twitch may serve shorter or longer;
  - answers `length`, `message` and `retry_after` (seconds before another);
  - refused with 400 (not live, or inside the cooldown) or 429 (inside the cooldown).
- **Get Ad Schedule** (`channel:read:ads`): `snooze_count`, `snooze_refresh_at`, `next_ad_at` (empty when none or not live), `duration`, `last_ad_at`, `preroll_free_time`. "A new ad cannot be run until 8 minutes after running a previous ad."
- **Snooze Next Ad** (`channel:manage:ads`): pushes the next automatic mid-roll back 5 minutes, if a snooze is left; answers the new schedule.
- **EventSub `channel.ad_break.begin` v1** (`channel:read:ads`): `duration_seconds`, `started_at`, `is_automatic`, and the requester.
- **The examples send numbers as strings, and times as RFC3339,** while some fields are described as Unix times. Every documented shape is read; anything else is an error.
- **Not offered:** an ad-ended event, an eligibility check, cancelling a break, which viewers saw an ad.

## Decision

### The truth model

- **Three outcomes for a request, never mixed up:**
  - *accepted*: Twitch took it. Not "an ad is running".
  - *refused*: nothing happened. Nothing is announced.
  - *unknown*: no clear answer (dropped connection, timeout, an unreadable 200). It may or may not have started.
- **A break is recorded only from Twitch's `channel.ad_break.begin`.** That's the only "ad is running".
- **The end is always an estimate:** start plus length, stored as `ads.break.end_estimated` with delivery quality *estimated*. An end due more than 5 minutes ago (StreamGremlin was closed) is marked, not announced.
- **Requests are never retried.** A second could start a second break.

### Storage (migration 0013)

- **`ad_requests`:**
  - id, `op_key` (unique), action (`commercial` or `snooze`), length, who asked;
  - status `pending`, `accepted`, `refused` or `unknown`, with reason and Twitch's message;
  - the accepted length, `retry_until`, the snooze's next ad and count;
  - `confirmed_by` (`event` or `schedule`), and the times.

  Triggers: an answered (accepted or refused) request never changes status, and nothing is deleted. Only an unknown one may later be found accepted.
- **`ad_breaks`:** key (Twitch's broadcaster and start time), start, length, automatic, the request it confirms, the estimated end, whether the end was announced. Never deleted.

### The service (`internal/ads`)

- **A request is recorded as pending before it's sent,** after StreamGremlin's own checks. Refused here, without asking Twitch (and not recorded, since nothing was asked):
  - a length outside 1 to 180;
  - another request of the same kind in flight;
  - inside the gap Twitch gave (`retry_until`);
  - an unknown commercial under 8 minutes old: a second could start a second break.
- **The same `op_key` returns the first request and its outcome,** without sending. Workflows use their step's idempotency key; the app and CLI make one per click or command.
- **Sent once,** with a 15-second limit that outlives the caller.
- **Its outcome is stored as an event** (source `core`, `ad_request:<id>`): `ads.break.requested`, `ads.snooze.applied`, `ads.request.refused` or `ads.request.unresolved`.
- **Breaks are followed from the inbox.** A manual break confirms the newest accepted or unknown commercial asked from 10 minutes before to 1 minute after it began.
- **At start, a request left pending becomes unknown** (reason `interrupted`), never resent.
- **Every 30 seconds, while an unknown commercial under an hour old waits,** the schedule's `last_ad_at` is checked. One within the same window is found accepted (`confirmed_by` `schedule`).
- **Test events never make breaks.**

### Steps (External, under More steps…)

- **"Run an ad break"** (seconds) and **"Snooze the next ad"**.
- **A refused or unknown outcome fails the step,** so later steps never announce an ad that isn't happening.
- **From chat, only the streamer's own message runs them.** A viewer's or moderator's would cost the streamer's viewers an ad nobody chose. Twitch itself lets only the broadcaster start one.

### Triggers and templates

- **Triggers:** "An ad break begins" and "An ad break should be over"; under More: Twitch accepts, refuses, an unclear outcome, a snooze.
- **Templates** (Ads group): "Tell chat about ad breaks" (speaks only once Twitch says the break began, and "should be over" at the estimated end) and "Run an ad from chat" (`!ad`, you only).

### Screen, API, CLI

- **API**, with permissions `ads.read` and `ads.write`:
  - `GET /v1/ads`: the schedule (or `state` saying why not), and the recent requests and breaks;
  - `POST /v1/ads/commercial {length, op_key}`: 200 accepted, 409 refused, 502 unknown;
  - `POST /v1/ads/snooze {op_key}`.
- **The Ads card on Home,** shown once Twitch says the channel has ads:
  - a running break with the time left, marked estimated;
  - the next ad, snoozes left, the last ad;
  - Run an ad now (length, then the app's dialog), disabled inside the gap; Snooze the next ad;
  - what was asked recently, in words: accepted, refused (Twitch's words), unclear.
- **CLI:** `streamgremlin ads`, `ads run SECONDS`, `ads snooze`.
- **mocktwitch** follows the documented rules: not live (400), the 8-minute gap (429 with `retry_after`), snoozes (3, +5 minutes, 429 when none), the break event with string-typed fields. `SetLive`, `HoldAdEvents`, `AdBreak` and `Commercials` drive it from tests.

## Alternatives considered

- **Retrying an unclear request:** it could start a second break. Checking the schedule instead.
- **Showing an ad as ended when the length passes:** Twitch doesn't say, and some viewers see ads later. It's an estimate, labelled.
- **Letting moderators run ads from chat:** Twitch doesn't let them run ads either; it's the streamer's call.
- **A separate Ads screen:** the card on Home is what FEATURES lists; the Ad Break Director is Later.

## Consequences

- **Not checked on real Twitch** until the channel is an Affiliate (D-039).
- **Sign in again once** for the three ad permissions.
- **An unknown request blocks another for 8 minutes** unless Twitch's event or schedule settles it sooner.
